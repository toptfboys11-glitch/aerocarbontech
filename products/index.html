Information that makes a quotation useful

A complete RFQ helps the factory separate confirmed requirements from assumptions before material and process options are discussed.

Technical inputs

  • 2D drawing or available 3D model
  • Dimensions, interfaces and critical tolerances
  • Application and operating environment
  • Surface, edge and appearance expectations

Commercial inputs

  • Prototype and expected production quantities
  • Required destination and target date
  • Inspection and documentation needs
  • Packaging or freight-forwarder requirements
