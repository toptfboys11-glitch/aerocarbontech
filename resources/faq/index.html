Carbon Fiber Product and RFQ Buyer FAQ

Use these answers to prepare a clearer carbon fiber sheet, rod, CNC component or OEM quotation request. Project-specific specifications and commercial terms are confirmed through the factory quotation.

Product selection

Define the application and critical characteristics before choosing a product label or appearance.

What is the difference between carbon fiber sheet and plate?

The terms often overlap in procurement. Compare actual thickness, laminate construction, flatness, finish and machining requirements rather than relying on the label alone.

How do I choose carbon fiber sheet thickness?

Thickness is only one design input. Review component geometry, unsupported spans, load direction, interfaces, stiffness targets and safety factors with the responsible engineer.

What surface patterns are available?

The repository shows woven carbon fiber surfaces, including twill imagery. Request current samples and written confirmation for any required weave or finish before ordering.

What is the difference between woven and unidirectional construction?

Woven reinforcement distributes fibers in more than one direction and can provide a recognizable surface. Unidirectional reinforcement concentrates fibers along a primary direction. The appropriate construction depends on the load path and laminate design.

Customization and drawings

Drawing-controlled inputs help separate required characteristics from negotiable ones.

Can carbon fiber sheets be cut to custom sizes?

Drawing-based outlines, holes, slots and cut-outs can be reviewed. Send the drawing revision and identify critical interfaces, tolerances and visible edges.

Can you manufacture from DWG, DXF or PDF drawings?

The existing site supports drawing-based review, but the repository does not contain an approved list of file formats. Email the available file and ask the team to confirm whether another format is required.

Can you drill holes, machine edges or add cut-outs?

The audited production content documents CNC, cutting and related in-house processes. Specific features, edge quality and tolerances require drawing review.

Can you produce OEM carbon fiber components?

Yes, drawing-based OEM component requirements can be reviewed. Material, process, sample and commercial assumptions are confirmed for the individual project.

Samples, MOQ and manufacturing

Avoid assuming universal quantities or schedules; these vary with component and process.

Can I order a sample?

A sample or prototype stage can be discussed. Feasibility, quantity, price and timing are confirmed in the quotation rather than promised as a universal policy.

What information is required for a sample quotation?

Provide the drawing or dimensions, application, critical characteristics, desired finish, sample quantity, destination and what the sample must validate.

Does MOQ depend on dimensions, thickness or tooling?

Yes, minimum quantity can depend on product form, material construction, dimensions, machining, tooling and setup. The website does not publish an unverified universal MOQ.

How is carbon fiber quality checked?

Inspection should be agreed around the drawing and order: dimensions, appearance, interfaces, quantity and any required records. The homepage also presents third-party factory audit evidence, but that is not a product-specific acceptance certificate.

Can prototypes be developed before recurring production?

A prototype or sample stage can be discussed when appropriate. Approval criteria, revisions and the transition to recurring production must be confirmed for the project.

Quotation, packaging and Middle East support

The existing conversion path remains RFQ form, email and WhatsApp.

What information is required for an accurate quotation?

Provide the drawing or dimensions, application, material assumptions, quantity, surface expectations, critical tolerances, inspection needs, destination and target timing.

What affects the final price?

Material construction, dimensions, machining complexity, finish, quantity, inspection, packaging and shipping requirements can all affect the quotation.

How are carbon fiber sheets protected during shipping?

Packaging should reflect surface sensitivity, edges, dimensions, quantity and shipping method. Ask the factory to confirm the proposed protection in the quotation; no universal pack specification is published here.

Can you ship through a courier or the buyer’s freight forwarder?

The homepage describes courier, air and sea options and freight coordination. Confirm the exact carrier, documents, destination and responsibilities for the order.

Can buyers contact the factory through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the website WhatsApp link for initial communication, then send drawings by email when file review is required.

Can samples be shipped to the UAE and surrounding markets?

The site supports Middle East project inquiries and export coordination. Shipment feasibility, courier, destination, cost and documentation must be confirmed for the specific quotation.

What drawing information helps the factory review CNC cutting?

Identify the finished outline, hole and slot relationships, datums, edge requirements, critical dimensions, drawing revision and which features are cosmetic or functional. See the carbon fiber machining capabilities.

How should I specify drilled holes in carbon fiber plate?

Show diameter, position, datum relationship, fastener or insert context, edge distance and whether the hole is a critical interface. The factory should review manufacturability without the website inventing minimum dimensions.

Can I send a 3D model with the 2D drawing?

Yes, an available 3D model can support geometry review, but the buyer should identify which document controls dimensions, tolerances and revision status. Confirm accepted file formats before transfer.

What should I compare between two carbon fiber quotations?

Compare the assumed product form, construction, dimensions, machining scope, finish, inspection, packaging, quantity, shipping responsibility and excluded items—not price alone.

Can the buyer’s freight forwarder collect the order?

The existing site supports freight coordination. Confirm collection location, packing information, documents, handover responsibilities and timing in the commercial agreement.

What is the inquiry process for UAE and Middle East buyers?

Start through the visible RFQ, email or WhatsApp channel. Share drawings, dimensions, quantity, application and destination; the team can then clarify technical assumptions, sample needs and shipment coordination.
