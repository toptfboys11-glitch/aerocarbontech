Carbon Fiber Resources for Better OEM Decisions

A practical knowledge center for engineering and purchasing teams comparing carbon fiber materials, manufacturing routes, specifications and supplier requirements.

Plan specifications with fewer assumptions

Carbon fiber performance depends on fiber orientation, resin system, geometry, process and service conditions. These topic groups are designed to help teams prepare clearer RFQs and identify the questions that should be resolved before production.

01 / MATERIALS

Material selection

Understand the tradeoffs between woven laminates, unidirectional reinforcement and common carbon fiber product forms before defining a drawing or sample.

02 / PROCESSES

Manufacturing methods

Compare pultrusion, roll wrapping, compression molding and CNC machining in terms of geometry, repeatability, tooling and practical production constraints.

03 / DESIGN

Design for manufacture

Review tolerances, wall thickness, holes, inserts, surface finish and edge details that can affect manufacturability and inspection.

04 / QUALITY

Quality and inspection

Prepare measurable acceptance criteria, drawing revisions, sampling expectations and documentation needs for a more controlled supplier review.

05 / SOURCING

OEM procurement

Build an RFQ package that communicates application, quantity, target delivery date and required evidence without relying on vague product labels.

06 / COMPARISONS

Product comparisons

Evaluate tubes, sheets, molded parts and machined components against functional requirements rather than treating every carbon fiber format as interchangeable.

What to include in a useful RFQ

A complete first inquiry helps engineering and sales teams identify the likely process and the information still needed for quotation.

Technical inputs

  • Drawing, dimensions and tolerances
  • Material or weave preference
  • Finish and cosmetic requirements
  • Application and service conditions

Commercial inputs

  • Prototype and production quantities
  • Target delivery date
  • Inspection or documentation needs
  • Destination and shipping expectations

This resource hub is an editorial foundation under technical review. Published guidance must be checked against the actual application, drawing and confirmed factory capability before it is used as a purchasing specification.
