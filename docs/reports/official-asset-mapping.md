## Usage boundaries

- Product images establish that the official catalog contains those visible product forms. They do not verify a universal specification, tolerance, MOQ, application approval or customer outcome.
- Tube imagery is used as product-form evidence on the Rod and GPS/RTK pages. It is not described as a finished surveying pole.
- CNC and sheet images on the Robotics page illustrate components that buyers can discuss. They are not presented as customer projects.
- No official packaging, inspection/QC or GPS/RTK-specific image could be verified with sufficient context during this pass. Existing repository factory, CNC and audit evidence remains in use where already appropriate.
