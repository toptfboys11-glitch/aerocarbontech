## Evidence and Permission Checklist

- [ ] Customer publication permission or documented anonymization approval.

## Required Original Information and Images

- Real requirement/problem; supplied inputs; verified material/process decision; QC/approval steps; sourced outcome.

1. H1: `[Verified component/project category] Case Study`
2. Context and constraints.
3. Inputs and selection.
4. Manufacturing/validation approach.
5. Verified outcome and limitations.
6. Related product/capability/guide.
7. CTA.
