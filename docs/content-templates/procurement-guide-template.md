# Procurement Guide Template

## Planning

- Objective, procurement intent, buyer role/stage, geographic relevance and indexation state:

## Required Original Information

- Buyer checklist, specification/RFQ fields, qualification evidence, quote comparison and risk questions.
- Company-specific policy only after approval.

## Required Images

- Packaging, documents, inspection or material examples with sensitive details removed and permission recorded.

## Suggested Structure

1. H1 focused on one procurement task.
2. Required specification information.
3. Supplier/factory evidence checklist.
4. Quote comparison factors.
5. Sample/approval process.
6. Packaging, documents and shipping considerations.
7. RFQ checklist and CTA.

## Links / CTA

- Procurement hub; product pillar; quality/export capability; RFQ.
- CTA: `Request Supplier Information` or `Start Your RFQ`.

## Schema

BreadcrumbList; Article after completion.

## Indexation / Verification

- [ ] Useful beyond a sales pitch.
- [ ] Current policy reviewed.
- [ ] Unique metadata/canonical/H1; links and sitemap QA.

## Legal / Factual Accuracy

- [ ] No invented MOQ, lead time, Incoterm, export document or guarantee.
