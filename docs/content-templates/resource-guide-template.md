# Resource Guide Template

## Planning

- Objective, informational intent, audience, primary question, conversion assist and indexation state:

## Required Original Information

- Reviewed definitions, decision criteria, examples and limitations.
- Distinguish general engineering information from verified Aero Carbon Tech capability.
- Cite authoritative external technical sources where needed.

## Required Images

- Original diagrams, samples or process photos; source/rights/alt/dimensions recorded.

## Suggested Structure

1. H1 answering one search topic.
2. Concise answer / decision summary.
3. Terms and options.
4. Selection/design considerations.
5. Common RFQ mistakes.
6. Practical checklist.
7. Relevant product/capability/application.
8. CTA.

## Links / CTA

- Parent resource cluster; relevant product; capability; application; RFQ.
- CTA should match the guide, e.g. `Request Sheet Guidance`.

## Schema

BreadcrumbList and Article only after editorial completion, real dates and authorship policy.

## Indexation / Verification

- [ ] Original depth, unique metadata/H1/canonical.
- [ ] Technical review complete.
- [ ] Not thin, duplicated or keyword stuffed.
- [ ] No fake date or author; sitemap only after QA.

## Legal / Factual Accuracy

- [ ] Claims sourced or qualified; no invented company specifications or guarantees.
