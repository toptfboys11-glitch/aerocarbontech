# Capability Page Template

## Planning

- Objective, intent, audience, proposed URL, parent pillar and indexation state:

## Required Original Information

- What the process is and which verified materials/products it applies to.
- Buyer inputs, accepted drawing/file requirements and review workflow.
- Design and quality-control considerations.
- Typical civil/commercial application categories.
- How feasibility and quotation are requested.

## Required Images

- Original process, equipment-in-context, in-process part and inspection evidence with rights and alt text.

## Suggested Structure

1. H1: `[Verified capability] for Carbon Fiber Parts`
2. Process scope.
3. Applicable products/materials.
4. Required buyer inputs/files.
5. Design considerations.
6. QC considerations.
7. Applications.
8. RFQ checklist and CTA.

## Links / CTA

- Capabilities pillar; relevant product; application; technical guide; `/\#contact`.
- CTA: `Send Your Drawing` or `Discuss Feasibility` only if supported.

## Schema

BreadcrumbList; Service only when the service is verified and accurately described.

## Indexation / Verification

- [ ] Unique metadata, H1 and canonical.
- [ ] Capability approved by business/production.
- [ ] No invented equipment models, tolerances, capacity or lead time.
- [ ] Proof/assets present; links valid; sitemap gated by QA.

## Legal / Factual Accuracy

- [ ] No unsupported process, inspection, certification, customer or outcome claim.
