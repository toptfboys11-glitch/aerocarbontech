# Comparison Page Template

## Planning

- Objective, comparison intent, audience, decision context and indexation state:

## Required Original Information

- Clearly defined materials/processes, comparable criteria, limitations and use-case tradeoffs.
- Sources for quantitative properties; avoid universal winner claims.

## Required Images

- Comparable samples/diagrams with equivalent framing and licensed use.

## Suggested Structure

1. H1: `[A] vs [B]: Selection Considerations`
2. Quick decision framework.
3. Definition and construction differences.
4. Criterion-by-criterion comparison.
5. Application fit and limitations.
6. Cost/procurement factors without fabricated pricing.
7. Questions for an RFQ.

## Links / CTA

- Comparison cluster; selection guide; relevant product/application; RFQ.
- CTA: `Discuss Material Selection`.

- [ ] Distinct intent; fair, sourced comparison.
