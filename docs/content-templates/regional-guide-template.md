# Regional Guide Template

## Planning

- Objective, country/GCC procurement intent, audience, buyer stage and indexation state:

## Required Original Information

- Distinct regional procurement, documentation, logistics, climate/application or communication considerations.
- Confirm all country-specific claims with current authoritative sources.

## Required Images

- Relevant licensed context or real packaging/logistics assets; never imply an unverified local office/customer/project.

## Suggested Structure

1. H1 identifying region and procurement task.
2. Buyer requirements in the region.
3. Relevant civil/industrial applications.
4. Specification and supplier qualification.
5. Documentation, packaging and shipping considerations.
6. RFQ checklist and CTA.

## Links / CTA

- Middle East hub; procurement guide; relevant product/application; RFQ.
- CTA: `Discuss Your [Region] Requirement`.

## Schema

BreadcrumbList; Article after completion. Do not use LocalBusiness unless a real local entity/location supports it.

## Indexation / Verification

- [ ] Distinct regional value; not a doorway template.
- [ ] Logistics/legal information reviewed and dated internally.
- [ ] Unique metadata/canonical/H1; sitemap after QA.

## Legal / Factual Accuracy

- [ ] No fake local presence, customer, project, delivery promise or regulatory advice.
