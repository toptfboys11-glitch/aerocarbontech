## Required Original Information

- Verified product definition and available variants.
- Verified dimensions, materials, weave/finish and supply constraints.
- Buyer inputs required for selection and quotation.
- Packaging, sample and order information only where policy is verified.

## Required Images

- Original product overview, surface/edge close-up, scale/dimension context and packaging/QC evidence.
- Record source, usage rights, alt text and dimensions.

## Suggested Structure

1. H1: `[Verified product name for B2B buyers]`
2. Buyer-oriented summary and primary CTA.
3. Verified options/specifications.
4. Selection considerations.
5. Applicable capabilities.
6. Application categories without customer claims.
7. Quality/proof.
8. RFQ requirements.
9. Related guides/products.

## Internal Links and CTA

- Parent Products pillar; applicable capability; application; selection guide; `/\#contact`.
- CTA: `[Request Product Quote / Send Dimensions]`.

## Schema Recommendation

BreadcrumbList. Product only when real product attributes support it; never invent offers, price, availability, ratings or reviews.

## Indexation Checklist

- [ ] Unique title/description/H1/canonical.
- [ ] Original and commercially useful.
- [ ] Verified data and assets.
- [ ] No duplicate variant page.
- [ ] Links resolve; sitemap only after approval.

## Business Verification

- [ ] Supply status, variants, MOQ/sample policy, accepted inputs and fulfillment scope approved.

## Legal / Factual Accuracy

- [ ] No invented tolerance, capacity, certification, lead time, client, test or performance claim.
