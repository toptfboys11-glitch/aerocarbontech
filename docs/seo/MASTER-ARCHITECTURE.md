# SEO Master Architecture — 12–24 Months

## Positioning and Guardrails

Aero Carbon Tech should be organized around carbon fiber sheets as the material/product foundation and custom sheet-derived parts as the commercial extension. The audience is B2B procurement, OEM, engineering and industrial teams, with primary geographic relevance to the Middle East/GCC.

Civil, commercial and industrial applications are in scope: UAV/drone inspection, surveying, environmental monitoring, robotics, automation, industrial equipment, automotive prototypes, marine/coastal equipment and lightweight assemblies. Military, weapon, targeting or combat content is out of scope.

No page may invent certifications, tolerances, machinery models, capacity, customers, delivery times, test results, case outcomes, pricing, reviews or ratings. Unverified offerings remain non-indexable until business approval.

## Pillar Model

### Carbon Fiber Sheets

- Proposed URL: `/products/carbon-fiber-sheets/`
- Supporting pages: carbon fiber plates; verified thickness/finish/weave variants; custom sizes; CNC-cut plates; sheet selection, thickness, size, weave and quality guides.
- Required inbound links: Products landing, relevant resources, application pages, capabilities.
- Required outbound links: CNC cutting, thickness guide, UAV/robotics/industrial applications, RFQ.
- Primary conversion: Request quote with dimensions, thickness, finish, quantity and application.
- Content gap: verified standard sizes, thickness availability, laminate construction, finish options and tolerances.
- Evidence: original product photos, dimension examples, finish samples, packaging, QC process.
- Phase: 1.

### Custom Carbon Fiber Parts

## Product Page Rules

Every product specification must state: proposed URL, intent, primary/secondary topics, buyer stage, H1, required sections/proof, CTA, links, parent, indexation and phase. Thickness, finish, weave and process variants are `NEEDS BUSINESS VERIFICATION` until real supply is confirmed. Avoid near-duplicate pages; consolidate variants where distinct specifications and buyer intent do not justify separate pages.

## Capability Page Rules

Each capability must explain the process, applicable products/materials, buyer inputs, drawing/file requirements, design considerations, QC considerations, applications and RFQ process. Do not publish tolerance numbers or equipment models without verified source material.

## Application Page Rules

Each application page links to a relevant product pillar, capability, technical guide and RFQ. Language must describe suitability considerations, not claim prior projects or operational performance. Regional relevance must add procurement/logistics/application value and must not become doorway content.

## Case Studies

`/case-studies/` is a future landing structure only. Drafts remain `noindex, follow` and out of sitemap. Required evidence: customer permission or documented anonymization approval, problem/input, verified process, measurable outcome source, images/files rights, confidentiality review and final business sign-off.

Potential categories: UAV frame prototype, robot chassis plate, industrial inspection bracket, custom panel and lightweight structural component. These are taxonomy examples, not claims that projects exist.

## Click Depth and Orphan Control

Every indexable page should be reachable within approximately three internal clicks from the homepage. Landing → pillar → support is the preferred maximum depth. Drafts may remain unlinked from global navigation, but must be reachable in Preview QA through an inventory list and must never enter the production sitemap.

## Phase 1 Implementation Decision

Only `/resources/`, `/applications/` and `/capabilities/` landing foundations are created in this task. They are `noindex, follow`, self-canonicalized to intended production URLs, excluded from sitemap and not added to the protected homepage navigation. They become indexable only after content/business review and approved merge strategy.
