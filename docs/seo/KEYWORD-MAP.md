## Cannibalization Decisions

| Overlap | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| carbon fiber sheet vs carbon fiber plate | One Sheets pillar initially; use a dedicated Plates page only if specifications and buyer language materially differ. | Avoid two near-identical commercial pages. |
| carbon fiber sheets manufacturer vs carbon fiber sheet supplier | Sections within the Sheets pillar plus one supplier-evaluation guide. | Manufacturer/supplier are overlapping commercial modifiers. |
| custom carbon fiber sheet | Section/supporting page under Sheets after custom-size capability verification. | Variant of the sheet intent. |
| CNC carbon fiber parts vs custom carbon fiber parts | Separate pages only if CNC sheet-derived parts and broader molded/custom components have distinct workflows and proof; otherwise one combined pillar. | Prevent commercial cannibalization. |
| carbon fiber drone frame | Product/application bridge; maintain a product page only for real frame products. | Product intent differs from material selection. |
| carbon fiber sheets for drones | UAV application/resource page linking to Sheets and CNC. | Application intent, not a duplicate product page. |
