# Content Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Safety and Planning

- Existing-site audit and homepage baseline.
- Preview workflow, QA and rollback plan.
- URL inventory, keyword map, internal-link map and indexation rules.
- Page templates and validation script.
- No major public-site changes.

## Phase 1 — Core Commercial Foundation

- Products landing page — after routing and product taxonomy approval.
- Carbon Fiber Sheets pillar — requires verified sizes, thicknesses, finishes and proof.
- Custom Carbon Fiber Parts pillar — requires drawing workflow and process evidence.
- Capabilities landing page — foundation drafted in this task, noindex.
- CNC Machining / Cutting capability — after business verification.
- Applications landing page — foundation drafted, noindex.
- UAV and Drone Applications pillar — civil/commercial scope only.
- Resources landing page — foundation drafted, noindex.
- Carbon Fiber Sheet Thickness Guide.
- Custom CNC Carbon Fiber Parts Guide.
- Request Quote changes only with separate approval if evidence shows the current form is inadequate.

## Phase 2 — Commercial Clusters

- Verified sheet/plate variants; avoid one page per dimension without distinct value.
- Robotics and automation applications.
- Industrial equipment and inspection systems.
- CNC design/CAD preparation/edge-hole design cluster.
- Supplier evaluation and RFQ cluster.
- Grouped FAQ hub.

## Phase 3 — Regional and Authority Building

- UAE procurement guide with local procurement/logistics value.
- Saudi industrial procurement guide.
- GCC engineering buyer guide.
- Packaging and shipping guide.
- Verified, permissioned case studies.
- Comparison pages and additional technical resources.

## Phase 4 — Data-Driven Expansion

Use Search Console and qualified inquiry data to decide which pages to expand, create, retitle, internally link, consolidate, noindex or remove. Do not prescribe arbitrary volume.

Recommended cadence: 2–4 substantial pages per month, only after business and technical review. Review performance quarterly.
